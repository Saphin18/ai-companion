"What's on your mind today?" is now the hero line — Fraunces 21 in the accent colour, sitting right under the greeting. It asks instead of announcing, which is the difference between a dashboard and a friend opening a conversation.

Rotate it, don't fix it. The same question every single evening becomes wallpaper within a week — the exact problem we just fixed in the chat replies. Vary it off data you already have:

  • default → "What's on your mind today?"
  • after a low week → "How's your head today?"
  • after a good streak → "What's good?"
  • first open in days → "Been a while. What's new?"
  • late at night → "Still up?"

On dropping the continue card — agreed for the default case, and the screen is calmer without it. One exception worth keeping: if they were chatting within the last hour, a single thin line under the question (“‹ back to this afternoon”) costs almost no space and saves someone who closed the app by accident. Your call — it's a two-line condition either way.

Everything else stays as you liked it: mood week strip, starter chips wired to tone_context, Goals and Journal tiles with live counts, input pinned at the bottom.

3. Voice — the cancel button

bug confirmed

It does exist — cancelRecording is wired to a trash icon at ChatInput.tsx:411. You couldn't find it because it is a thin outline glyph on a low-contrast background with no label, placed next to text shouting “tap to send”. That is a discoverability bug, and it is worse than a missing feature: people hold a recording they don't want and send it by accident.

Now — what you saw

Three controls, none labelled, two of which do the same thing (the pill and the send button both stop and send). No timer, no waveform, no sign it is even picking up sound.

🗑 Recording... tap to send ➤

Cancel is the dim glyph on the far left.

Proposed — recording

Cancel becomes a labelled button with a danger outline. A live waveform proves the mic is working, and a timer shows how long they have (you auto-stop at MAX_RECORD_MS and currently never warn them).

× Cancel 0:12 ➤

Word, not icon. Nobody has to guess.

Proposed — review before send

The bigger win. Stop no longer sends — it gives them the clip back to play, delete or send. Right now the only way to check a voice note is to send it.

▶ 0:12
🗑 Delete Send ➤

Hold-to-talk also works: release sends, slide up cancels — but only with a visible cancel, never instead of one.

Three rules this follows. A destructive action gets a word, not a glyph — an unlabelled trash icon is a guess. One control, one job — today the pill and the send button are duplicates while cancel hides in the corner. And never send media the user hasn't heard back.
My recommendation: Landing option C, the revised home exactly as drawn, and all three voice changes — the labelled cancel is twenty minutes of work and removes a real "I sent that by mistake" moment.
