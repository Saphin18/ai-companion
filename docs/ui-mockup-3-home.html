Home — how much belongs here?

You asked whether Goals and Journal need to be on home when they already live in the drawer. Short answer below, then three versions so you can see the difference.

The rule I'd use: never duplicate navigation — surface state.

A tile that says “Goals →” is the drawer link again with a bigger tap target. It earns nothing and it costs a third of the screen. But a line that says “Sleep by 12 — 4 days running” tells you something you didn't know, and that earns its place.

So you're half right. The duplication is pointless. But deleting them outright has a real cost too: behind a hamburger, Journal, Goals and Reminders effectively do not exist for most people, and those three are the only reason this is more than a chat app. A feature nobody sees doesn't get used, and one nobody uses may as well not be built.

Three versions

same tokens, same type

All three keep your greeting, the question, the chips and the input bar. They differ only in how much sits between.

Option A

Bare

Greeting, question, chips, input. Nothing else. Closest to a person opening a conversation, furthest from an app. The drawer keeps everything else.

What the contextual line says

option B detail

One line, first match wins, nothing if none match. Every condition below runs off data you already store — no new tables.

WhenShowsFrom
A reminder is due todayCall mum · 7:00 pmreminder_repository
A goal has a streak of 3+Sleep by 12 · 4 days runninggoal_repository
Mood logged low 3 days straightRough few days. Want to talk about it?mood_repository
Chatted in the last hour‹ Back to this afternoonchat_repository
Journal untouched 7+ daysNothing written in a while.journal_repository
None of the above— nothing renders — 
The thing that makes this work is the last row. Most home screens show every widget every day, so people stop seeing any of them. If this line is absent four days out of five, the day it does appear you'll actually read it. Same principle as the quote gate we just built into the chat — rare is what makes it land.

So should Goals and Journal stay in the drawer?

yes

With two changes.

ItemWhereWhy
JournalDrawer only Deliberate, occasional. Nobody journals by accident, so nobody needs a shortcut — they came for it.
GoalsDrawer + streak line Lives in the drawer, but a streak is time-sensitive and worth one line on home.
RemindersDrawer + due-today line A reminder you can't see has failed at its only job. This is the strongest case of the three.
About / WallpaperMove into Profile Settings, not features. They're padding the drawer and making the real items harder to find.
MoodHome only You log it already and the user never sees it. Nowhere else to put it.
Second change: label the drawer honestly. Right now it is six items in a flat list with Wallpaper sitting next to Goals. Two groups — Yours (Journal, Goals, Reminders) and Settings (Profile, Wallpaper, About) — makes the features findable without putting anything extra on home.
Recommendation: Option B. Mood strip always, one contextual line when it has earned it, no fixed tiles. Goals and Journal stay in the drawer; move About and Wallpaper into Profile and group what's left.
